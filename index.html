A house in the old town since 1896

( Lækjargata 3, Innbærinn, Akureyri )

A pale green timber house at the foot of Lækjargata, in Innbærinn, the oldest part of Akureyri. Built in 1896 and rebuilt on new foundations a century later, it holds the highest preservation value in town. Inside are three apartments, each with its own kitchen and bathroom, and the house’s collection of paintings.
