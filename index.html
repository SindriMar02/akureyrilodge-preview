A historic house. Your own place in Akureyri.

( Lækjargata 3, Innbærinn, Akureyri )

A characterful timber house dating back to 1896, with three individual apartments, which can also be combined — each offering the comfort and independence of having your own place.

Stay for a few nights, explore Akureyri and North Iceland at your own pace, and come home to a quieter corner of the city.

A house with a story to tell

( Since 1896 )

Some places are simply somewhere to sleep. Others have a character all of their own.

Akureyri Lodge is a timber house built in 1896 in Innbærinn, Akureyri’s oldest neighbourhood. Its walls have witnessed generations of local life, and its history remains part of the experience today.

The house is home to three individual apartments, with paintings from its collection adding character to the interiors.

It is a place where historic surroundings meet the comforts of an independent stay — with the town outside your door and room to relax when you return.

Akureyri on your doorstep

( Innbærinn, the old town )

Located in the historic neighbourhood of Innbærinn, Akureyri Lodge puts you within walking distance of the town’s museums, cafés, restaurants and cultural attractions.

Spend the morning exploring the old streets, visit the botanical garden, head into town for dinner, or discover the landscapes beyond Akureyri. Goðafoss, Lake Mývatn and Húsavík make rewarding excursions, while geothermal bathing and whale watching offer memorable ways to experience North Iceland.

Whether you stay for a few days or use Akureyri as a base for a longer journey, there is plenty to discover.

Akureyri Lodge is part of a small collection of distinctive Icelandic properties owned by Stella & Eyþór.

Their journey into hospitality began with Hotel Laugar and grew from a shared interest in places with character, a sense of place and the ability to make people feel at home.

Akureyri Lodge reflects that philosophy: an individual historic house, thoughtfully prepared for travellers who appreciate comfort, independence and a little more character than an ordinary overnight stay.
