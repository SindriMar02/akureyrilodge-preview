Explore Akureyri and beyond

Start in the old town. Discover North Iceland from there.

Akureyri Lodge is situated in Innbærinn, a historic neighbourhood where much of the town’s character can be discovered on foot.

Take a walk through the old streets, visit local museums, explore the botanical garden or make your way into the centre for cafés, restaurants and shops.

When you are ready to venture further, Akureyri is a convenient starting point for exploring the landscapes and experiences of North Iceland — from geothermal bathing and whale watching to waterfalls, volcanic landscapes and memorable day trips.

Use the map below to plan your time, whether you prefer exploring on foot or setting out by car.

Discover the historic streets and attractions of Akureyri, with museums, the botanical garden and the town centre all accessible on foot.

Around the town, 4 to 11 minutes from the house by car.

Venture beyond the town to experience geothermal baths, waterfalls, coastal communities and the landscapes around Lake Mývatn.

Distances and travel times are approximate and may vary with road conditions and your chosen route. Walking and driving times from Lækjargata 3, measured by route in Google Maps. East of town the drives go through the Vaðlaheiði tunnel, which has a toll.

( Our favourites )

Akureyri on your doorstep

Akureyri is the capital of North Iceland and a destination worth more than a quick stop.

The town offers museums, galleries, shops, cafés, restaurants, bars, swimming pools and cultural events throughout the year.

From Akureyri Lodge, the centre is just over one kilometre away and easily reached on foot.

Spend the morning exploring town, have lunch at a local café, head out into the surrounding countryside in the afternoon and return to Akureyri for dinner.

Or simply stay a little longer at your home away from home.

Explore North Iceland

Akureyri is also an excellent base for discovering the wider region.

From here, you can explore some of North Iceland’s best-known natural attractions, including Goðafoss, Lake Mývatn, Húsavík and the surrounding landscapes of North Iceland.

Depending on the season and your interests, there are opportunities for:

  • Whale watching
  • Hiking
  • Horse riding
  • Golf
  • Geothermal swimming
  • Fishing
  • Nature and sightseeing
  • Museums and local culture
  • Restaurants and cafés

You don’t have to choose between being close to nature and having a town around you.

At Akureyri Lodge, you can have both.
